ingredients:
- slug: all-purpose flour
  amount: 1 1/2 C
- slug: cornstarch
  amount: 1/2 C
- slug: sugar
  amount: 4 tbsp
- slug: baking powder
  amount: 1 tsp
- slug: baking soda
  amount: 1/2 tsp
- slug: kosher salt
  amount: 1/2 tsp
- slug: buttermilk
  amount: 2 C
- slug: vegetable oil
  amount: 1/4 C
- slug: eggs
  amount: 2
- slug: vanilla
  amount: 1 1/2 tsp
- slug: butter
  amount: 1/4 C
  notes: melted

---

1. Preheat oven to 250°. Place a wire baking rack in a sheet pan and set in oven. Your waffles will rest here as you finish cooking each one.
2. Combine flour, cornstarch, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl and set aside.
3. In a large measuring cup whisk buttermilk, oil, eggs, and vanilla.
4. Add wet ingredients to dry and stir until just combined. Drizzle in butter while continuing to mix. Allow batter to rest for 10 minutes while you heat up your waffle maker.
5. Pour about ¾ cup of batter onto preheated waffle maker, or the amount suggested by your waffle maker directions. Cook to manufacturer instructions, or about 4-5 minutes.
6. Transfer the cooked waffles to the wire rack and keep warm in the preheated oven as you cook the rest. Repeat to cook the remaining batter.
