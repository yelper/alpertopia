1. Mix the flour, instant yeast, sugar and salt in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook. Add the warm milk and egg and mix on low until a shaggy dough forms. Knead on medium for 6 minutes, then add the softened unsalted butter a tablespoon at a time and knead 4 to 5 minutes more, until smooth, soft and elastic. Cover and leave in a warm place until doubled, about 1 hour.
2. Beat the very soft salted butter with the 6 grated garlic cloves and the black pepper until smooth and spreadable. It must be soft enough to spread thinly without tearing the dough.

3. Knock back the dough, cover and rest 10 minutes. On a lightly floured surface, roll it with a rolling pin to about 16 x 12 inches, cover and rest 5 minutes, then roll on to a 24 x 16-inch rectangle, about 3/16 inch thick, with a long side facing you. Check the size with a ruler.
4. Spread all of the garlic butter right to the edges with an offset spatula, leaving a ½-inch strip bare along the far long edge. Scatter the grated Black Bomber evenly over the butter. Roll up snugly from the near long edge into a 24-inch log, keeping the turns tight; you should get about five. Pinch the seam closed and lay the log seam-side down.
5. Chill the log on a tray for 15 minutes to firm the butter. Slide a length of unflavored dental floss or strong thread under the log, cross the ends over the top and pull to cut 12 buns, each about 2 inches wide.
6. Butter a 9 x 13-inch metal pan and arrange the buns cut-side up in four rows of three, slightly apart. Cover and leave to rise until puffed and touching, 45 to 60 minutes. Heat the oven to 375°F.
7. While the buns rise, melt the 4 Tbsp salted butter over low heat, stir in the 2 grated garlic cloves and cook 1 minute without browning. Take off the heat and set aside.
8. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, until deep golden and the centers read about 195°F on an instant-read thermometer. Let the cheese at the cut edges catch to golden bits; tent loosely with aluminum foil only if the tops darken before the centers are done.
9. Stir the parsley and chives into the warm garlic butter and brush it generously over the buns with a pastry brush as soon as they come out of the oven. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then serve warm, pulled apart at the table.
