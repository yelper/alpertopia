1. Pour all ingredients in a mason jar or sealable container. Shake/stir until all ingredients are mixed.
2. Place oats in refrigerator to chill overnight. When ready to eat, enjoy hot or cold.
3. Note: I prefer my breakfasts to be lower in sugar, so this might not be sweet enough for your tastes. You can add more maple syrup or fruit to taste!
